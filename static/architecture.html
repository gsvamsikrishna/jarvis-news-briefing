End-to-end system

The browser is the interaction surface. FastAPI coordinates local persistence, search, selection, speech, and model calls. Choose a component in the map to inspect its role.

  • External data leaves the laptop only for Exa search/content requests and browser-managed speech recognition where the browser provider requires it.
  • Ollama, profile data, and macOS speech synthesis run locally on the laptop.
Privacy boundary: topic names are sent to Exa as search queries; selected article text and the profile name go to Ollama locally. Browser speech recognition may use its configured speech service. Opening publisher links contacts those sites directly. Microphone audio goes to browser speech recognition, not FastAPI.
